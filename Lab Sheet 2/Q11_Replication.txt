MongoDB Primary-Secondary replica set. Requires separate mongod processes with same replSet name and different ports/dbpaths.
Start two configured instances, then connect to primary:
mongosh --port 27017
rs.initiate({_id:'rs0',members:[{_id:0,host:'localhost:27017'},{_id:1,host:'localhost:27018'}]})
rs.status()
use ReplicationDB
db.Students.insertOne({Student_ID:1,Name:'Aarav'})
Connect to secondary:
mongosh --port 27018
rs.secondaryOk()
use ReplicationDB
db.Students.find()
An eligible secondary may be elected primary if the primary becomes unavailable. Three voting members are recommended for production-like fault tolerance.
