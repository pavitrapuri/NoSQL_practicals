MongoDB sharding demonstration. Requires multiple shard replica sets, a config-server replica set and mongos router; not a single script.
After configuring and starting these processes, connect to mongos and run:
sh.addShard('shardA/localhost:27101')
sh.addShard('shardB/localhost:27201')
sh.addShard('shardC/localhost:27301')
sh.enableSharding('ShardDB')
use ShardDB
db.Students.createIndex({Student_ID:'hashed'})
sh.shardCollection('ShardDB.Students',{Student_ID:'hashed'})
for(let i=1;i<=20;i++){db.Students.insertOne({Student_ID:i,Name:'Student'+i,Course:'BCA'});}
sh.status()
Ports and replica-set names are examples and must match your configuration. Benefit: data/workload distribution across servers.
